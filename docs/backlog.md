# Backlog

Post-hackathon ideas, in no particular order. Nothing here is promised, scheduled or approved. Each item
needs its own handoff before any work starts, and the non-negotiables in AGENTS.md apply to all of them.

## Game

- **The walkable Mint and Your Lot.** Today the Vault ends at "PENNY IS AWAKE" and a Mint door that says
  "Your Lot opens soon." The follow-up is a place the player can walk around: the Mint, and Your Lot
  where awake robots live.
- **The other four robots and their wake rules.** Lumen (Spotlight), Quill (Freelance), Axle (Delivery) and
  Barter (Selling) are sleeping silhouettes. Each needs its own wake rule tied to real, matched work in
  its app, written and tested the way Penny's tile rules are.
- **A story arc per robot.** Each robot gets its own short arc that plays out as the player's real record
  grows. Story only; nothing here can be bought.
- **Loading screens with tales of the Mint.** Replace the boot log shown after login with short tales that
  fit the console look.
- **Vault awakening stats.** Add Grit, Shine, Range and Craft progress bars using real streak, matched
  share, income app count and best hourly rate. Until an approved server payload carries those fields,
  keep the awakening state to "PENNY IS AWAKE" and the Mint door.
