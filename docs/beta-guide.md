# OneDime beta guide

This is a test version. Records may be reset, so keep anything you need elsewhere.
Use fictional names, amounts and notes. Do not upload client files, study content,
real PayPal activity, passwords or other private work.

## Log in

Open `<BETA_URL_FROM_BRANDON>`, which Brandon sends with this guide. The first visit
after a quiet spell can take up to a minute to wake up. Choose **Log in with PayPal**
and use only the sandbox test account Brandon sends privately. Do not use your real
PayPal login. If the account does not work, tell Brandon; do not create another one.

## What to try

1. **First Dime:** log a fictional work session with time and earnings. Edit it, then
   check Quests. Logged earnings can earn XP; a ladder rung needs a matched payment.
2. **Spotlight:** add fictional creator income and a brand deal. Move the deal through
   its stages to paid and check the totals.
3. **Freelance:** add a fictional gig with hours and a fee. Change its payment stage
   and check the net and hourly amounts.
4. **Market:** log a delivery shift with miles, then a sale with item cost and shipping.
   Check the Delivery and rides and Selling tabs.
5. **PayPal:** import only the fictional sample CSV Brandon provides. Review a payment,
   choose its workspace and link it once. Check that it does not count twice.
6. **PayPal:** change the month in the overview, then use **Export everything**. Open
   the downloaded CSV or Excel file and compare it with your test records.
7. **On your usual device:** try light and dark mode, then move among all five apps.
   If you want to test Smart suggestions, read the download notice before turning it
   on in PayPal. You still confirm each suggestion; it never moves a payment for you.

OneDime never does gig work or logs into a gig platform for you. Payouts remain
disabled during this beta.

## Send feedback

Choose **Menu > Send feedback**, or **Send feedback** in First Dime's Help tab.
The current app is selected for you; change it if needed. In **What happened**, say
what you clicked, what you expected and what you saw. Keep it under 1,000 characters
and leave out private information. Tick **This blocked me** if you could not finish
the task, then send it. You will see a thank-you; other players cannot read your note.

You can send five notes per hour. If login or the form itself is broken, send Brandon
the same details through the private contact route he gave you. Do not send a password.
