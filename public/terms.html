Terms

OneDime is a private, free play-test for adults aged 18 and over. It helps you track your own independent work. Players pay nothing. There are no ads and no selling your data.

Your work and records

Only upload your own records and PayPal activity. Keep amounts accurate and choose one workspace per payment. Do not upload another person's private information unnecessarily, interfere with other accounts, or use OneDime to automate, scrape or log into gig platforms. Never automate the work.

Suggestions and earnings

Smart suggestions run on your device and can be wrong. You decide each route; the server checks it. Logged earnings may match a PayPal payment's gross or net when payer/platform agrees. CSV matching is not an authenticated PayPal lookup and does not prove the file is genuine. Only verified First Dime earnings count toward rung eligibility and public earnings rankings. Other apps earn no XP or quests.

The tracker does not promise income, jobs, payment delivery or prizes, and does not provide tax, financial or legal advice. PayPal is sandbox only. Milestone bonuses are disabled by default and require separate approval; sandbox balances are not real money. OneDime does not charge for AI or require a paid AI account.

Your choices

You may leave the leaderboard, turn Smart suggestions off, export your records, or delete your account and owned active records in PayPal settings by typing DELETE MY DATA. Export before deleting if you want a copy. See Privacy for storage and sharing details.

As a play-test, features may change or be unavailable. Keep copies of records you need. Contact Brandon Caris through your play-test invitation channel with questions or to report a problem.
