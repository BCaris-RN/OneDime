Privacy

OneDime is a private play-test for adults aged 18 and over. Brandon Caris operates it. Contact Brandon through the channel where you joined the play-test.

What is stored

OneDime stores your PayPal login identity and available name, email and verification status; time zone and settings; work records, private notes, income, costs, clock state, XP, quests and rung claims. Session cookies keep you signed in. The database stores hashed session tokens, never your PayPal password.

PayPal CSV uploads are parsed and discarded. Only date, payer, gross, fee, net, currency, transaction ID and payment type are kept, with your confirmed record link. Sandbox bonus state and recipient details may be stored if that separately approved feature is used.

Who can see it

Records belong to your account. Joining the leaderboard is optional: it shares your display name, verified earnings and platform totals, XP, level and rung claims. It never shares your email or notes. Account and activity data support the features you choose; no ads, no selling data and no advertising trackers.

Smart suggestions

Smart suggestions are optional and off by default. Model files download from OneDime and are cached by your browser. Payer, amount and payment kind are processed on your device, with no AI service receiving them. Your imported activity remains stored in OneDime. The preference is saved per account; the browser also remembers your theme.

Export and delete

Use Export in any app, or Export everything in PayPal, for CSV or Excel files. Settings in PayPal has Delete my data: type DELETE MY DATA to remove your account and owned active database records and end your session. Records are otherwise kept for your account's use. Downloaded exports remain on your devices. Anonymous webhook event IDs may remain for duplicate protection. Ask Brandon about any independently retained backups.

PayPal handles its own login service under its own policies. This play-test uses sandbox PayPal.
