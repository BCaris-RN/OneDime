            The one rule

            Automate everything around the work, never the work. All five sites pay for a human doing the task and actively catch bots and AI-written answers. A ban costs the unpaid balance and the account. Every tip below makes you faster instead.

            The daily loop

            • Start of your day: check available studies when you have time to complete them carefully.
            • Gaps between studies: transcription on Rev or TranscribeMe. Keep Prolific alerts on so you can switch the moment a study appears.
            • Evening: a second Prolific check, then SideShift content in one batch.
            • Microworkers: only when nothing else is available, and capped at 20 minutes.
            • Every session: clock in, clock out, log it. The streak counts time-only days too.

            More money for less time

            • Choose the file, not the queue. On transcription, preview the first 30 seconds. Take clear audio with one or two speakers and skip heavy accents, crosstalk and noise. You're paid per audio minute, so a bad file can cut your hourly rate by more than half.
            • Text expansion. Map short codes to the tags you type all day, such as [inaudible], [crosstalk] and speaker labels. macOS Text Replacements or the free tool espanso both work.
            • Learn the editor hotkeys for play, pause and rewind 2 seconds until your hands never leave the keyboard. Then buy a USB foot pedal with your earnings, not before.
            • Typing speed compounds. Ten minutes a day on a typing trainer. Every added word per minute raises every transcription hour from then on.
            • Prolific screeners. Answer every About You question. More completed screeners means you qualify for more studies, including studies relevant to your experience.
            • Return fast, never rush. If a study is broken or underpays, return it right away. Returning doesn't hurt you. Failing attention checks does.
            • SideShift as an assembly line. One fixed filming spot, one light, one script skeleton (hook, demo, why it helps, call to action). Film three takes in one sitting and reuse the pitch message to brands.
            • Weekly cut. Every Sunday, check the per-hour table. Drop time from whichever site sits at the bottom.

            Back office on autopilot

            • One PayPal account and one personal email across every site.
            • Turn on every payout notification so deposits come to you instead of you checking dashboards.
            • Keep income and expense records, and check the tax rules that apply where you live.
            • Keep site passwords in a password manager. Never use a tool that auto-accepts tasks or fills in answers.

            What OneDime is

            OneDime is a quest game and scoreboard for remote gig work. You log what you earn and how long it took on sites like Prolific, Rev, TranscribeMe, SideShift and Microworkers. It turns that into goals, streaks, levels and an honest per-hour ranking of every site you use.

            The idea is simple: the hardest dollar is the first one. The first goal is a single dime, so you get a win early. Then the ladder keeps you climbing, and the numbers show you where your time is actually worth the most.

            Get started

            Use Log in with PayPal to sign in. OneDime currently uses PayPal sandbox accounts.

            1. Open Quests and work through the setup quests for each site. Checking one off gives you XP right away.
            2. When you sit down to work, go to Log, pick the site, and press Clock in.
            3. When you stop, press Clock out. Whole minutes fill in for you, rounded down. A session under one minute fills in 0. Type what you earned and press Log it.
            4. Check the Playbook for the daily routine and ways to earn more per hour.

            How each part works

            • The coin shows progress toward your current goal. Goals go $0.10, $1, $10, $50, $100, $250, $500, $1,000, and the next one unlocks as soon as you pass the last.
            • Streak counts days in a row with at least one entry. Days where you worked but earned nothing count too, using Log time only, $0. Approvals and tests take time before money shows up, and that effort still counts.
            • Level and XP: 1 XP per minute worked, 10 XP per dollar earned, plus each quest's reward, 100 XP per rung claimed, and any rested bonus. Total XP is rounded down to a whole number. Each level takes a little more XP than the last.
            • Which site pays you best divides what you earned by the time you logged on each site. Only sessions with minutes count, so time your sessions for honest numbers.
            • Rested XP: your pool starts at zero. After your first logged day, each full day without an entry adds 25, capped at 100. Minutes earn double while the pool lasts, using one pool XP per bonus XP.
            • History shows your recent entries. Use Delete to fix a mistake, then log it again.

            The leaderboard

            • Joining is optional. When you join, other players see your display name, verified total, XP, level and verified earnings by site. Your rung claims are also visible. Your email, log entries and notes stay private.
            • Bonus quests: each rung of the goal ladder ($0.10, $1, $10 and up) can be claimed once, by the first player whose verified total reaches it. Claiming one is worth +100 XP.
            • Rankings show the top earners overall and the top earner on each site.
            • Verified earnings only: logged entries await verification before they count toward rungs or public earnings. A rung claim stays with its winner, including after deleting an entry or leaving the leaderboard.             Choose the right workspace

            Needs an audience? Spotlight. Selling a skill to a client? Freelance. Driving, delivering or selling goods? Market. Everything else, including SideShift, is First Dime. Every payment belongs in exactly one tab. Printify and Ninja Transfers are suppliers; Printify Shop is a store.

            Use your exports

            Every workspace exports CSV or Excel files, with all-time, month or date-range filters. In Google Sheets choose File > Import > Upload, then select the file. Open .xlsx directly in Excel, or use Data > From Text/CSV for CSV. Market workbooks contain Delivery and Selling sheets; each includes its expenses. Export everything from PayPal for a monthly Summary and a sheet per table, or a ZIP of CSV files. Values ending in _usd are dollars. Text that could become a spreadsheet formula starts with a protective apostrophe.

            Questions

            • Does it connect to my gig accounts? No. You enter everything yourself. That keeps your logins private and keeps you inside every site's rules.
            • Can it do the work for me? No, on purpose. These sites pay for a human and ban accounts that use bots or AI answers. OneDime makes you faster instead.
            • Where is my data? Your log, quests, time zone and running clock live in OneDime's database, linked to your PayPal sandbox identity. Only your light or dark theme preference is stored in this browser. Other players cannot read your entries or notes.
            • What if I close the page while clocked in? The timer keeps counting. Come back and clock out whenever you finish.
            • Is the per-hour number before taxes? Yes. Gig income is usually self-employment income, so set some aside from every payout.
